Flowity AI · São Paulo
The short version

I build external brains for companies. Not dashboards, not chatbots: the layer between what a company already knows and what it actually decides. Businesses drown in their own signals, reviews, support threads, meeting notes, documents, market noise, and almost none of it reaches a decision in usable form. Closing that gap is my company, my research, and most of my waking hours.

Flowity AI is the commercial side: a premium done with you service for B2B SaaS teams. The five open systems on this site are the public side: the same machinery, free, documented, and installable by anyone.

The research

My research formalizes the practice. The framework is called Executive Signal Synthesis: it treats signal synthesis as its own layer between data processing and decision making, with four stages, ingestion, classification, clustering, and synthesis. It is published as an open preprint, and the five systems are the framework running in the wild.

How I work

In public, on my own stack, with receipts.

Everything I ship, I run first. My company's memory lives in Continuum. Its documents answer through Canon. Its big calls go through Council and come out as permanent decision records. When one of my systems fails me, you'll hear about it before you hear the pitch.

I publish the numbers, the mistakes, and the decisions as they happen. Not because transparency is a brand strategy, but because signal literacy is the whole point: if I can't read my own company honestly, I have no business reading yours.
