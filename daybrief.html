Questions

Which edition fits me?

Start with Make if you want the brief to arrive by itself and you would rather not touch anything technical. Pick Claude if you already pay for Claude Pro and like typing /daybrief at your desk. Pick n8n if you already run n8n. You get all three, so you can switch any time.

How long does setup take?

Claude edition: about 20 to 30 minutes. Make edition: about 30 to 45 minutes. n8n edition: about 45 to 90 minutes. A video and a written guide walk you through every step.

Does it run when my computer is off?

The Make edition and n8n Cloud do, because they run in the cloud. Self hosted n8n needs a machine that stays on. The Claude edition runs when you open Claude Code and ask for your brief.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You import a scenario or a workflow, or copy a folder, then connect your accounts and paste a couple of keys. The videos show every click.

What do I need to run it?

Your own tools: Claude Pro for the Claude edition, a Make account and a free Groq account for the Make edition, or n8n plus a free Groq account for the n8n edition.

Can you see my email?

No. Daybrief runs on your own accounts and I never see your data. The Claude edition stays within your Claude account. The Make and n8n editions send email text to the AI provider you choose. If you want nothing to leave your machine, self host n8n with Ollama.

What if I get stuck?

Email me. You get 30 days of email support, then the troubleshooting page and the docs.
