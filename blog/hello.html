Why I'm building in public

Saying the numbers out loud, even when they are small.

For a long time I built quietly. I would finish something, polish it, and only then show it to anyone. By the time it was ready, I had no idea whether anyone wanted it.

So I am doing it the other way now. I share what I am working on while I am working on it: the tools, the decisions, the numbers, and the mistakes.

What that means here

  • I write about what I am actually building, not what I plan to build someday.
  • I share real numbers, even the small ones.
  • When something does not work, I say so.

If you are building something on your own, I hope it helps to see someone else do it in the open. And if you want the weekly version, The Letter is where I write it first.
