Free: the one page template I use to read a company before a first conversation

A one page template to understand a company from public records only, so your first message or meeting starts with something true about them.

Before I write to a company, I read it from the outside. Public records only. It takes a short while and it changes the whole conversation, because I can open with something real about them instead of something about me.

This is the template. Free, copy it.

The one page

Company: name, site, what they sell, who they sell to.

1. What customers repeat Source: reviews from the last two quarters (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot, app stores). Write the one complaint that appears most, and link three reviews that say it.

2. Where the team is stretched Source: careers page and job posts. Which roles are open, how long, and what the descriptions emphasise.

3. What they had to change Source: pricing page today versus six months ago (Wayback Machine). One change, and what it might mean.

4. What breaks again and again Source: public changelog or release notes. The area that gets patched repeatedly.

5. What I could not see Write this honestly. Revenue, internal decisions, what they already know.

6. One question to ask them Not a pitch. A question that shows you did the work. For example: I noticed onboarding comes up a lot in recent reviews, is that something you are already working on?

Rules that make it work

  • Every line needs a link. No link, it does not go on the page.
  • One page only. If it does not fit, you have not decided what matters.
  • The question at the end is the point. The page exists to make it a good one.

I use this before every first message. If you try it, tell me what the template missed. I will update it.
