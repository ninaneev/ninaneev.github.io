What it is

Your company's knowledge is scattered across contracts, decks, notes, exports, and a folder called final_v3. When you need an answer, you either dig for twenty minutes or ask an AI that confidently makes something up. Neither is knowledge. One is archaeology, the other is fiction.

Canon ingests your documents into a local index and answers questions over them. Every answer comes with receipts: the exact file, the exact passage it drew from. If it can't find a source, it says so instead of inventing one. That single rule changes what you can trust it with.

It runs on your machine, against your files. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, which means it works for the documents you'd never paste into a chat window: client contracts, financials, research drafts.

The name is literal. A canon is the body of texts a tradition treats as authoritative. That is what your documents become when every answer has to cite them.

What you get
  • A local ingestion pipeline: point it at folders, it builds the index
  • Question answering over your documents with inline source citations for every claim
  • An honest "not found": it never fills gaps with fiction
  • Support for the formats real work lives in: PDF, Markdown, text, Office documents
  • Full documentation: setup, reindexing, tuning retrieval for your corpus
