Compass · Stage 01 · it listens

Your market is talking about you. Compass writes it down.

What it is

The most honest information about your company is not in your dashboard. It's in reviews of your product and your competitors, in support threads, in Reddit posts, in who your rivals are hiring and what your market complains about when nobody official is listening. Almost nobody reads it systematically, because raw signal is exhausting.

Compass makes your AI scan those public sources on a schedule, classify what it finds, cluster the noise into patterns, and deliver a weekly signal brief: what changed, what it means, and the sources behind every claim. No invented trends. If a pattern has two data points, the brief says it has two data points.

It is the front door of the whole stack, and the most direct implementation of my research: the ingestion and classification stages of Executive Signal Synthesis, running on public data you already have the right to read.

What you get
  • A source map for your company and competitors: reviews, forums, app stores, job posts, news
  • A scan routine your AI runs, with classification, clustering, and evidence grades built in
  • The weekly signal brief: patterns with data point counts, and a source for every claim
  • An honest "no signal this week": it never invents a trend
  • Feeds Council's Signal Reader seat, so outside voices sit in on your decisions
  • Full documentation: choosing sources, tuning relevance, avoiding echo chambers
