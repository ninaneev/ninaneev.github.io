AI work

AI that does a real job inside your business, with your data, your rules and a person checking what matters.

  • Claude systems

    Claude set up as part of your team: skills, commands and memory built around your real workflows, so it knows your offers, your clients and your way of writing.

    For example · a proposal writer that knows your prices and past wins · a weekly review that reads your CRM and inbox · a research desk that keeps every source and date

  • AI assistants

    Assistants that answer from your own documents and hand the conversation to you when they should, instead of guessing.

    For example · a website assistant that answers buyers and books calls · an internal helper for policies and processes · inbox triage that drafts replies for you to approve

  • AI agents

    Agents that carry a task through several steps on their own and stop for your approval before anything leaves the building.

    For example · a lead research agent that finds and qualifies prospects · a support agent that sorts and routes tickets · a content agent that drafts from your notes and calls

Development work

The pieces between your tools and the pages your clients see.

  • Automations

    The repetitive work between your tools, done for you every time, with a log you can check.

    For example · form leads straight into your CRM with a follow up email · invoice and payment reminders · a Monday report in your inbox or Slack

  • CRMs

    A CRM shaped to how you actually sell, in Airtable, Notion or your own app, not a template you bend to fit.

    For example · a pipeline with stages and reminders · a client portal view · an AI summary of every account before a call

  • Landing pages

    One page, one offer, written and built to get the next step: a call, a signup, a sale.

    For example · an offer page with checkout · a waitlist with a welcome email · an event or webinar page

  • Web apps

    Tools that run in the browser, on any computer or phone. I don't build native mobile apps.

    For example · a client portal · an internal dashboard · a booking or intake tool
