About

Every company already knows the answer. It just can't remember it.

Businesses drown in their own signals: reviews, support threads, meeting notes, documents, market noise. Almost none of it reaches a decision. I build the layer in between. Flowity AI is an external brain, a premium done with you service for B2B SaaS teams that turns what a company already knows into decisions people can act on.

Everything I build, I build in public: the systems, the numbers, the mistakes. If you want to watch a company being assembled one system at a time, you're in the right place.
