The week, day by day

  1. Day 1

    Pick what you sell

    List what people already ask you for and choose the one you enjoy delivering.

  2. Day 2

    Choose who it is for

    Name one buyer and the problem they would pay to have gone.

  3. Day 3

    Fix the scope

    Decide exactly what is in, what is out and how long it takes.

  4. Day 4

    Set the price

    Use the pricing calculator to land on a number you can say out loud.

  5. Day 5

    Write the offer page
